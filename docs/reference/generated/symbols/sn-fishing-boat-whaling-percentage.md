## `sn-fishing-boat-whaling-percentage`

- Kind: `strategic-number`
- Detail: SN 316 - Water

The percentage of fishing ships which will gather from whales. All other ships will gather from food fish.

Default: `-1`

Required range: `0 to 100`

Range: `0 to 100`
