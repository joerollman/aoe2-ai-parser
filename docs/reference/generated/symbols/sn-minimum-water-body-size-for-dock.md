## `sn-minimum-water-body-size-for-dock`

- Kind: `strategic-number`
- Detail: SN 112 - Water

The minimum number of tiles (in surface area) that a body of water must be for a Dock to be placed on it.

Default: `300`

Required range: `10 to Max`

Range: `Min to Max`
