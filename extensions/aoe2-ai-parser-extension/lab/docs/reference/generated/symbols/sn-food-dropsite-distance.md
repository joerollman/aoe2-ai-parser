## `sn-food-dropsite-distance`

- Kind: `strategic-number`
- Detail: SN 163 - Economy

The maximum number of tiles a computer player likes to walk to drop off its food.

Default: `3`

Required range: `3 to 255`

Range: `Min to Max`
