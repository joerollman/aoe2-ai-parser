## `sn-camp-max-distance`

- Kind: `strategic-number`
- Detail: SN 86 - Buildings

Sets the maximum distance that lumber camps and mining camps may be placed from a Town Center.

Default: `25`

Required range: `7 to 255`

Range: `Min to Max`
